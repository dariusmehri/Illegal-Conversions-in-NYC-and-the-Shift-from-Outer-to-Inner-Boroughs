## Illegal Conversions in NYC: The Shift from Outer to Inner Boroughs (1990s-Present)
Illegal conversions are unauthorized changes to a building—like turning basements, attics, or single-family homes into multiple apartments—without approval from the New York City Department of Buildings, often violating fire and safety codes. They matter because they significantly increase fire and risk of serious injury and death: buildings end up overcrowded, with unsafe wiring, blocked exits, and layouts unknown to responders from the Fire Department of the City of New York. This combination—hidden occupants, poor egress, and substandard conditions—makes conditions in dwellings more likely to become severe or fatal, which is why illegal conversions are a critical signal in identifying high-risk buildings.
